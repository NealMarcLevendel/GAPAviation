GAP AVIATION - COMMANDS, KEYS AND CONTROLS
==========================================

LAUNCHING (Terminal, in the GAPAviation folder)
-----------------------------------------------
./run.sh                    Start Minecraft with all three mods (+ Immersive Vehicles)
./run.sh --dh               ...also with Distant Horizons
./run.sh build              Build release jars of all three mods into dist/
./run.sh <gradle args>      Extra Gradle options, e.g.:
    -PdhVersion=<version>       Use a different Distant Horizons version (with --dh)
    -PdhDefaults                Don't apply the Mac-safe Distant Horizons settings (with --dh)


IV AUTOPILOT
============

Chat commands
-------------
/autopilot fly <runway>                       Fly to a runway: takes off first if you're lined up on a
                                              runway, then route, circuit, approach, landing, rollout.
                                              Anywhere on a runway (crooked, off the centerline, facing
                                              the wrong way) it lines up by itself first.
                                              Use quotes for names with spaces: /autopilot fly "KJFK 09"
                                              (Tab completes runway names.)
/autopilot fly <runway> <altitude>            ...at this cruise altitude (Y level)
/autopilot fly <runway> <altitude> <km/h>     ...and this cruise speed
/autopilot taxi <gate|runway>                 Taxi along the taxiway network to a terminal gate, or onto a
                                              runway and line up there (Tab lists both). Works from a gate,
                                              a taxiway or another runway: on a runway it rolls along it to
                                              the exit that leads there and turns off. /autopilot fly from a
                                              gate taxis out to the nearest runway by itself, lines up and
                                              takes off; after landing it taxis to the nearest free gate.
... delay <seconds>                           Add to fly or taxi: the plane stays parked this long, then
                                              starts by itself (on the ground only; touching the controls
                                              cancels). e.g. /autopilot taxi "Gate 2" delay 60
                                              /autopilot fly R1 3000 600 delay 30
/autopilot off                                Disengage the autopilot (works from anywhere aboard)
/autopilot runways                            List runways: heading, length and marker coordinates
                                              (click a coordinate to copy it)
/genwaypoints <radius> <blocks>               (Op) Lay out the taxi network around you: waypoints along the
                                              middle of every taxiway/apron made of these blocks within the
                                              radius (8-2048), linked, with runway entrances where taxiways
                                              meet a runway, and existing terminal gates hooked on.
                                              Blocks: comma-separated, e.g.
                                              /genwaypoints 200 gray_concrete,yellow_concrete
                                              Stand on the airport's ground level. Runways are left out
                                              automatically. (/genWayPoints works too.)
/genwaypoints airport <blocks>                (Op) Same, for the whole airport you're standing in (its
                                              rectangle, up to 8192 blocks a side).
                                              Both run in the background (the ground is read a few chunks
                                              per tick), with a progress bar at the top of the screen.
/genwaypoints entrances                       (Op) Make every taxiway dead end next to a runway an entrance
                                              to it (done automatically when generating; use after adding
                                              runway markers or waypoints by hand).
/genwaypoints cancel                          (Op) Stop a generation that's running.
/genwaypoints clear <radius>                  (Op) Remove the plain taxi waypoints in the radius (keeps
/genwaypoints clear airport                   terminal gates), or in the whole airport, e.g. to regenerate.

Keys (rebindable: Options > Controls > IV Autopilot)
----------------------------------------------------
G               Cycle mode: Off -> Pitch hold -> Altitude hold -> Off
Up / Down       Pitch hold: target pitch +/- 1 degree.  Altitude hold: target altitude +/- 5 blocks
O               Auto-trim on/off (when flying by hand, trim slowly takes over the elevator you hold)
(The keys work from the pilot seat, or anywhere aboard while the autopilot is engaged.)

Taking over
-----------
Touching the flight controls during automatic takeoff, flare or rollout hands the plane to you.

Blocks (creative tab "IV Autopilot")
------------------------------------
Runway Marker   Rename in an anvil (e.g. "KJFK 09"); place one at each end of the runway centerline.
                Shows a floating label: name, heading, length, coordinates.
                Right-click: show/refresh the runway's status.
Taxi Waypoint   Flat marker for taxiways (planes roll over it). Rename in an anvil (e.g. "Gate A2")
                to make it a gate/stand. Right-click: show its links.
Taxiway Wand    Right-click a waypoint, then another waypoint: link (or unlink) them; keep clicking
                to chain a taxiway. Waypoint then Runway Marker: that waypoint becomes the runway
                end's entrance/exit. Sneak + right-click in the air: clear the selection.
                While held, the network shows on the ground (yellow taxiways, cyan runway links).
                Tip: for big jets, round sharp corners with an extra waypoint or two.
Terminal Gate   Where planes park (light blue). Gets a name by itself ("Gate 1", "Gate 2", ...) unless
                the item was renamed in an anvil, and links itself to the nearest taxi waypoint.
                Placing a plain Taxi Waypoint also links it automatically: to the nearest waypoints
                within 48 blocks (one per direction, only across flat open ground), splitting a link
                if you place it on one, and making it a runway entrance if it's next to a runway.
                The wand still links/unlinks by hand. Turn off: taxi.autoLinkDistance = 0.
Airport Marker  Place two at opposite corners of the airport: the rectangle between them is the
                airport (runways, taxiways and gates can all be inside it). The second one joins the
                nearest airport still missing a corner; or name both the same in an anvil. Unnamed
                ones are called "Airport 1", "Airport 2", ... Right-click: show the airport.
                Planes departing from it keep their wings level (rudder turns) until they leave it
                or climb above its ceiling; arriving/landing planes are never restricted by it.
                (Airports from before this change have one marker each: add the opposite corners.)

Traffic control (automatic, for autopilot aircraft)
---------------------------------------------------
Runways         One aircraft at a time. Departures hold short at the runway entrance waypoint (or wait
                lined up) until the runway is free; arrivals get priority. Arrivals line up in order of
                reaching the circuit; if the runway is busy they hold at the approach entry, stacked
                30 blocks apart. Anything on the runway during final = go around.
Taxiways        Each plane reserves the waypoints just ahead; others stop short of them and stop behind
                any vehicle in their path. Blocked 20 s by a reserved waypoint: re-routes if it can.
                Tip: give busy areas a second way round (or one-way loops), or a head-on meeting on a
                single taxiway has to wait for you.
In the air      Two autopilot planes within 300 blocks and 60 high: the one further from landing climbs
                60 blocks until clear.
After landing   Leaves the runway at the first waypoint ahead that leads to a free gate (any
                waypoint linked to the runway's marker or within 40 blocks of the centerline counts),
                then taxis to the nearest free gate. If it already rolled past every exit, it taxis back to the
                nearest. Turn off: landing.taxiToGate.

Plane tracker (needs JourneyMap, included in the test instance)
---------------------------------------------------------------
J               Open JourneyMap's full-screen map: every aircraft in the world is shown (also on the
                minimap), with its planned autopilot path and destination runway. Hover for details.
                Green = autopilot to a runway, yellow = pitch/altitude hold, white = hand-flown,
                grey = parked / last seen (not loaded). Turn off: tracker.enabled in the server config.
/planetracker                 Show the tracker's map settings (client-side, works anywhere)
/planetracker size <8-128>    Plane icon size on the map, pixels (default 36)
/planetracker text <0.5-4>    Label text size (default 1.75)
/planetracker labels on|off   Labels under the planes (hovering still shows details)
                              Saved in instance/config/ivautopilot-client.toml

Settings
--------
instance/saves/<world>/serverconfig/ivautopilot-server.toml
(pitch, altitude, bank, throttle, takeoff, landing, circuit, cruise, stall protection, no-bank zones,
 chunk loading ahead of aircraft)
Flight recorder: one [FDR] line per second in instance/logs/latest.log while the autopilot is on.


GAP CABIN WALK
==============

Controls
--------
Shift (while seated)   Stand up and walk around inside the aircraft (on the ground or in flight)
Right-click a seat     Sit back down (normal Immersive Vehicles seat)
N                      Head inertia on/off (rebindable: Options > Controls > GAP Cabin Walk)

Chat commands (client-side: work in singleplayer and on any server, no permissions needed)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/headinertia                        Show the current head-inertia settings
/headinertia <0-5>                  Set turning + acceleration intensity together (1 = default)
/headinertia turning <0-5>          Head lag when the vehicle rolls / pitches / yaws
/headinertia acceleration <0-5>     Head nod from braking, speeding up and bumps
/headinertia roll <0-10>            Extra lag when rolling into / out of a bank (default 2)
/headinertia tilt on|off            Bank your view with the cabin while walking around
/headinertia on|off                 Same as the N key

Settings
--------
instance/config/gapcabinwalk-client.toml   (head inertia: strength, roll, acceleration, neck stiffness,
                                            damping, max angle, tilt when standing)


GOLDEN AEROTRANSPORT (1.20.1 port)
==================================
No commands of its own: the aircraft appear in the Immersive Vehicles creative tabs and are flown
with the normal Immersive Vehicles controls.
