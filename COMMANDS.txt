GAP AVIATION - COMMANDS, KEYS AND CONTROLS
==========================================

LAUNCHING (Terminal, in the GAPAviation folder)
-----------------------------------------------
./run.sh                    Start Minecraft with all three mods (+ Immersive Vehicles)
./run.sh --dh               ...also with Distant Horizons
./run.sh build              Build release jars of all three mods into dist/
./run.sh <gradle args>      Extra Gradle options, e.g.:
    -PdhVersion=<version>       Use a different Distant Horizons version (with --dh)
    -PdhDefaults                Don't apply the Mac-safe Distant Horizons settings (with --dh)


IV AUTOPILOT
============

Chat commands
-------------
/autopilot fly <runway>                       Fly to a runway: takes off first if you're lined up on a
                                              runway, then route, circuit, approach, landing, rollout.
                                              Use quotes for names with spaces: /autopilot fly "KJFK 09"
                                              (Tab completes runway names.)
/autopilot fly <runway> <altitude>            ...at this cruise altitude (Y level)
/autopilot fly <runway> <altitude> <km/h>     ...and this cruise speed
/autopilot off                                Disengage the autopilot (works from anywhere aboard)
/autopilot runways                            List runways: heading, length and marker coordinates
                                              (click a coordinate to copy it)

Keys (rebindable: Options > Controls > IV Autopilot)
----------------------------------------------------
G               Cycle mode: Off -> Pitch hold -> Altitude hold -> Off
Up / Down       Pitch hold: target pitch +/- 1 degree.  Altitude hold: target altitude +/- 5 blocks
O               Auto-trim on/off (when flying by hand, trim slowly takes over the elevator you hold)
(The keys work from the pilot seat, or anywhere aboard while the autopilot is engaged.)

Taking over
-----------
Touching the flight controls during automatic takeoff, flare or rollout hands the plane to you.

Blocks (creative tab "IV Autopilot")
------------------------------------
Runway Marker   Rename in an anvil (e.g. "KJFK 09"); place one at each end of the runway centerline.
                Shows a floating label: name, heading, length, coordinates.
                Right-click: show/refresh the runway's status.
Airport Marker  Optional no-bank zone around it (wings level, rudder-only turns).
                Rename in an anvil to name the airport.
                Right-click: radius +50.   Sneak + right-click: radius -50.

Settings
--------
instance/saves/<world>/serverconfig/ivautopilot-server.toml
(pitch, altitude, bank, throttle, takeoff, landing, circuit, cruise, stall protection, no-bank zones)
Flight recorder: one [FDR] line per second in instance/logs/latest.log while the autopilot is on.


GAP CABIN WALK
==============

Controls
--------
Shift (while seated)   Stand up and walk around inside the aircraft (on the ground or in flight)
Right-click a seat     Sit back down (normal Immersive Vehicles seat)
N                      Head inertia on/off (rebindable: Options > Controls > GAP Cabin Walk)

Chat commands (client-side: work in singleplayer and on any server, no permissions needed)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/headinertia                        Show the current head-inertia settings
/headinertia <0-5>                  Set turning + acceleration intensity together (1 = default)
/headinertia turning <0-5>          Head lag when the vehicle rolls / pitches / yaws
/headinertia acceleration <0-5>     Head nod from braking, speeding up and bumps
/headinertia roll <0-10>            Extra lag when rolling into / out of a bank (default 2)
/headinertia tilt on|off            Bank your view with the cabin while walking around
/headinertia on|off                 Same as the N key

Settings
--------
instance/config/gapcabinwalk-client.toml   (head inertia: strength, roll, acceleration, neck stiffness,
                                            damping, max angle, tilt when standing)


GOLDEN AEROTRANSPORT (1.20.1 port)
==================================
No commands of its own: the aircraft appear in the Immersive Vehicles creative tabs and are flown
with the normal Immersive Vehicles controls.
